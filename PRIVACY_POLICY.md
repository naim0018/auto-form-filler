# Privacy Policy for Auto Form Filler

**Last updated:** September 9, 2026

Auto Form Filler is a browser extension designed to help developers and testers fill web forms with randomly generated test data.

## Data Collection

Auto Form Filler does not collect, store, sell, or transmit personal information or user data.

## Random Test Data

All test data generated by the extension is randomly created for form-testing purposes. The generated data is not based on the user's personal information.

## Website Access

The extension may access the active webpage when required to detect form fields and insert randomly generated test data.

This access is used solely to provide the extension's form-filling functionality.

The extension does not collect, store, or transmit the contents of webpages or form fields.

## Data Sharing

Auto Form Filler does not sell, transfer, or share user data with third parties.

## Remote Servers

Auto Form Filler does not transmit generated form data or webpage content to external servers.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the extension's functionality or data practices change. Any changes will be reflected on this page.
